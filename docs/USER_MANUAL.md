# User Manual

For researchers and student researchers using TAIRI DataHub.

## Accounts and roles

- **Researcher** — browse, search, view metadata, request access, download after approval.
  Register yourself at **Create account**.
- **Student Researcher** — everything a researcher can do, plus upload and manage your own
  datasets and approve access requests to them. These accounts are created by an administrator.
- **Guest** — anyone not signed in can browse, search and read metadata, but cannot download
  or request access.

## Signing in

1. Click **Sign in** and enter your email and password.
2. First-time accounts should verify their email via the link that was sent.
3. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
4. Change your password anytime under **Profile → Change password**.

## Browsing and searching

- Use the search bar (top navbar or landing page) to search by title, author, keyword.
- On **Browse**, filter by research area, license and file type, and sort by newest, most
  downloaded, most viewed, or alphabetical.

## Requesting access to a dataset

1. Open a dataset page and click **Request access**.
2. Fill in the popup: purpose, institution, research area and an optional message.
3. Submit — the dataset owner is notified.
4. You'll receive an email and an in-app notification when a decision is made.
5. Once approved, return to the **Files** tab and download.

## Uploading a dataset (student researchers)

1. Go to **Dashboard → Upload**.
2. Fill in the metadata: title, description, authors, license, research area, tags, visibility.
   - **Visibility options:** Public (anyone can download), Restricted (download on approval),
     Public metadata (details visible, files on request), Private (only you).
3. Drag & drop files of any type — there are no format or size restrictions.
4. Click **Publish**. Your dataset enters **pending approval** until an administrator reviews it.

### Versioning

Uploading additional files from the **Edit** page creates a new version (e.g. 1.0 → 1.1).
Older versions remain accessible on the dataset's **Versions** tab.

## Managing your datasets

- **Dashboard → My datasets** lists your datasets with downloads, views and storage.
- **Edit** to update title, description, license, tags, README and visibility.
- **Delete** performs a soft delete; administrators can restore it if needed.

## Reviewing access requests to your datasets

1. Go to **Dashboard → Access requests → Incoming**.
2. Click **Review** on a pending request.
3. Choose an access level (view only / download / download + API) and duration
   (permanent / time-limited), add an optional note, then **Approve** or **Reject**.

## Citing datasets

On any dataset page, open the **Citation** tab to copy an APA, IEEE or BibTeX citation, or
download a `.bib` file.

## Notifications

The bell icon shows real-time notifications (new requests, approvals, downloads, comments).
Full history is at **Dashboard → Notifications**.
